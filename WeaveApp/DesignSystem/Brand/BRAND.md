# Weave logo: usage guide

**Woven Rings.** Three rings, each passing over one ring and under the next. No one carries the group alone: remove any one ring and the other two fall apart. There is one ring per member, in WeaveTheme mint, periwinkle, and peach.

Everything here is generated by `source/make_logo.py`. Don't edit the SVGs by hand; change the script and regenerate (see **Regenerating** at the end).

## Colour

| Name | HEX | RGB | CMYK (approx.) | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mint | `#8EDCBC` | 142 220 188 | C35 M0 Y15 K14 | top ring |
| Periwinkle | `#A3ADFF` | 163 173 255 | C36 M32 Y0 K0 | right ring |
| Peach | `#FFC98F` | 255 201 143 | C0 M21 Y44 K0 | left ring |
| Ink | `#111111` | 17 17 17 | C0 M0 Y0 K93 (use rich black for large areas) | tile, wordmark on light |
| Brand green | `#2D6A4F` | 45 106 79 | C58 M0 Y25 K58 | app icon, one-colour mark on light |
| Paper | `#F1F0EC` | 241 240 236 | C0 M0 Y2 K5 | light backgrounds |

The CMYK values are straight conversions. Proof them before printing, and have a printer match Pantone references from a physical swatch.

**Contrast decides where the colour mark goes.** The pastel rings reach 9–12.6:1 on ink but only 1.5–2.1:1 on white.

- **Ink or brand green background:** full-colour rings.
- **White or Paper background:** one-colour rings, in ink or brand green (6.4:1 on white).
- **Photos or busy backgrounds:** use the symbol on its ink tile.

## Lockups

- **Horizontal:** the wordmark's x-height is 0.48 × the symbol height. The gap between symbol and wordmark is 0.32 × the symbol height.
- **Stacked:** the wordmark's x-height is 0.30 × the symbol height, centred under the symbol.
- Don't rebuild lockups by hand. Use the files, which lock these proportions.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space:** keep at least the diameter of one ring's opening (≈ 0.45 × the symbol height) free on every side.
- **Symbol:** use the master at 40 px and up. From 16 to 39 px, use the small cut (heavier bands and wider gaps, so the weave survives). Below 16 px, don't use the mark.
- **Horizontal lockup:** at least 96 px wide (24 mm in print).
- **Wordmark alone:** at least 64 px wide.

## Wordmark

"weave" is custom-drawn, not set in a font, so there's no licence restriction. It's built from heavy geometric strokes: diagonals about 27 units wide, bowls 26, and the e-bar 20 (horizontal strokes are drawn thinner so they look equal), on a 100-unit x-height. Round letters overshoot the baseline and x-height by 1.5 units.

In the app, UI text stays in SF Pro (see `WeaveTheme`). Apple's licence covers SF Pro in app interfaces, but not in logos or marketing artwork, so always use these wordmark files for the logo itself.

## Don't

- Put full-colour rings on white or Paper. They wash out.
- Change the ring order (mint top, periwinkle right, peach left) or recolour individual rings.
- Fill in the weave gaps, or redraw the crossings without the over/under pattern.
- Add outlines, shadows, gradients, or glows.
- Stretch, rotate, or set the wordmark in another typeface.
- Use the master symbol below 40 px. Switch to the small cut.

## Regenerating

From the repo root:

```sh
python3 WeaveApp/DesignSystem/Brand/source/make_logo.py WeaveApp/DesignSystem/Brand/svg
```

This writes every SVG. PNGs, web icons, and the presentation were rendered from those SVGs with the logo-design skill's `render_png.py`, `export_variants.py`, and `presentation_board.py`, using headless Chrome. Any SVG renderer works (`rsvg-convert`, Inkscape, cairosvg).

App icon PNGs must be 1024 × 1024 with no alpha channel: render `svg/appicon-*.svg` and flatten them onto black before replacing the files in `AppIcon.appiconset`.
