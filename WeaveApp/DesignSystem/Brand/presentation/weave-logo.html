What we heard

The brief

Study groups try to keep each other off their phones, but limits are solitary and asking for more time means explaining yourself. Weave gives the group a shared time budget: going over costs everyone a little, and your reason stays private unless you choose to share.

togethersupportiveprivatecalmbold

Success: says 'together' without people clip-art, avoids the clock/hourglass/lock clichés of screen-time apps, matches the app icon, works in one colour and at 16 px.

Final design

Woven Rings

Three rings, each over one and under the next: no one carries the group alone.

  • Borromean weave: remove any one ring and the other two fall apart, which is the group
  • Mint, periwinkle and peach come straight from WeaveTheme; one ring per member
  • Same geometry as the app icon, generated by one script
  • Custom-drawn heavy wordmark matches the app's heavy SF Pro UI without using SF Pro
